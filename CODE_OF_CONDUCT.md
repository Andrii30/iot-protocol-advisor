# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the
work. Harassment or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a confidential issue or contacting the
maintainer. Reports will be reviewed and handled as promptly as possible.
